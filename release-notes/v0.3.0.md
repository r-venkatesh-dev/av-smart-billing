# AV Smartbilling Desktop v0.3.0

AV Smartbilling is an offline-first billing workspace for creating invoices, managing customers and inventory, recording payments, and reviewing business performance from your computer.

## What's new

- Bulk Excel tools: Download templates, export, and import products and customers with automatic duplicate matching and stock movement tracking.
- Audio feedback: Barcode scanner beeper and warning sounds for Quick POS.
- Streamlined software access: Removed admin control center options from the billing software workspace for shop owners.
- Improved Quick POS with barcode input, held bills, walk-in customers, payment selection, and pay-and-print shortcuts.
- UPI payment QR support during checkout and on invoices with an outstanding balance.
- Sales reports with date filters and CSV, Excel, and PDF exports.
- In-app software update / version check against the cloud platform.
- Application lock with a six-digit PIN, inactivity timeout, and Touch ID where supported.
- Versioned encrypted cloud backups with selectable restore points and local safety copy.

## Downloads

Choose exactly one installer for your computer:

- **Windows 64-bit:** `AV-Smartbilling-Setup-0.3.0.exe`
- **Mac with Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4 or newer):** `AV-Smartbilling-0.3.0-arm64.dmg`
- **Mac with an Intel processor:** `AV-Smartbilling-0.3.0-x64.dmg`

After downloading, open the installer and follow the prompts. Existing users should create a cloud backup before updating.
